WOLK Coffee & Cakes, Richmond

Questions

Short answers about visiting, brunch, coffee and cakes at 16 Hill Rise.

Visiting

Where is WOLK Coffee & Cakes?

16 Hill Rise, Richmond, London TW10 6UA. On Hill Rise, between Richmond Bridge and Richmond Hill.

What are the opening hours?

Every day, 7:30am–5:30pm.

How far is WOLK from Richmond station?

About 10 minutes on foot.

Do I need to book?

No. WOLK is walk-in only, including weekend brunch.

Is WOLK dog friendly?

Yes. Dogs are welcome.

Is there Wi-Fi? Can I work from WOLK?

Yes. There is Wi-Fi and laptops are welcome when the shop isn't busy.

Is there outdoor seating?

Yes. There are tables outside.

Is the entrance step-free?

Yes. There is no step at the entrance.

Brunch & food

When is weekend brunch served?

Saturday and Sunday only, 8am–2pm.

Do you serve brunch on weekdays?

No. Brunch is Saturday & Sunday, 8am–2pm. On weekdays, Morning at WOLK is served until 2pm: toasts and croissant sandwiches.

What is menemen?

A Turkish egg dish: slow-cooked tomato and pepper with soft-set eggs. Ours comes with feta and sourdough.

What is çılbır?

Turkish poached eggs on strained yoghurt with chilli oil, served with sourdough.

What are syrnyky?

Ukrainian curd cheese pancakes, served with seasonal jam, honey and soured cream.

Is there a vegan option?

Yes. Avocado on Toast is vegan, and oat and coconut milk are available for drinks.

Where can I find allergen information?

Ask our team before you order.

Coffee & drinks

What coffee do you serve?

Espresso drinks, batch brew, single origin pour over and Turkish coffee.

Do you have decaf and plant milks?

Yes. Decaf coffee, oat milk and coconut milk.

Do you use flavoured syrups?

Only our own. Syrups and infusions are made in house.

Do you serve Turkish coffee and Turkish tea?

Yes. Turkish coffee is £3.50 and Turkish tea is £2.00.

Do you sell coffee beans?

Yes. Bags to take home start at £12.00.

Cakes

Do you have Basque cheesecake?

Yes. San Sebastián cheesecake, also called Basque cheesecake, is baked on site and sold by the slice at £7.00.

Can I order a whole cake?

Yes. Order at wolkcakes.co.uk with 48 hours' notice and collect from the shop.
